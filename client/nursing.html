What is Nursing?

Secure your seat in India's premier institutions through our professional counseling network.

Nursing is a noble and highly respected healthcare profession focused on the care of individuals, families, and communities so they may attain, maintain, or recover optimal health and quality of life. Nurses are the backbone of the healthcare system, working closely with doctors and other medical professionals to ensure patients receive continuous and compassionate care.

A nursing education involves comprehensive training in medical sciences, patient care techniques, anatomy, pharmacology, and clinical procedures. Students learn through both classroom instruction and extensive practical experience in hospitals and clinics. Modern nursing also involves the use of advanced medical technology, electronic health records, and research-based practice.

Choosing a career in nursing requires empathy, resilience, excellent communication skills, and a strong commitment to serving others. It is a highly rewarding profession that offers opportunities to make a direct positive impact on people's lives every single day.

Scope of Study

  • B.Sc Nursing, Post Basic B.Sc Nursing
  • General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM)
  • Auxiliary Nursing Midwifery (ANM)
  • M.Sc Nursing and specializations (Pediatric, Psychiatric, Medical-Surgical)
  • Clinical practice and hospital training

Job Opportunities

  • Registered Nurse / Staff Nurse
  • Nursing Supervisor / Superintendent
  • Clinical Instructor / Nurse Educator
  • Public Health Nurse
  • Critical Care / ICU Nurse
